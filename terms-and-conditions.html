Please read these terms before creating your student account. By ticking the acceptance box on the registration page, you confirm that you have read and agree to these conditions.

1. About UNI-VERSE

UNI-VERSE is an academic resource platform intended to help students access course notes, past questions, downloadable documents, videos and other learning materials. You agree to use the platform for lawful academic and educational purposes.

2. Account registration and information

To register, you must provide your full name, matriculation number, email address, department and a password. You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide is accurate and belongs to you. Keep your password confidential and do not share access to your account.

3. Student verification

New student accounts are initially marked Unverified. You can access the core academic resources available to unverified users, including course notes, past questions and PDF downloads. Access to videos and AI-powered learning materials is reserved for verified accounts.

To request verification, sign in and open the verification page displayed for your unverified account. Verification is completed online by uploading a clear and readable image of your university student ID card.

The ID image is requested only so an authorized UNI-VERSE administrator can compare the submitted identification details with the information on your account and decide whether to approve the request. Submitting an image does not automatically mean that your account has been verified.

4. Review, approval and rejection

After you submit your ID image, your request is marked Pending while an authorized administrator reviews it. The administrator may approve the request or reject it if the image is unclear, incomplete, inconsistent with your registration details or otherwise insufficient for verification.

If your request is rejected, you will be able to review the status and submit a replacement image. Your account remains subject to the unverified access restrictions until approval is recorded.

When your request is approved, your account status changes to Verified, the verification prompt/page is removed from your student interface, and the additional verified-student features become available.

5. Use, access and deletion of ID images

Your uploaded ID image is stored in a private storage area and is intended to be accessible only to authorized administrators responsible for verification. It must not be made publicly accessible or used for unrelated purposes.

Once your verification is approved, the uploaded ID image will be deleted from the active UNI-VERSE storage system.

If a request is rejected or remains pending, the image may be retained temporarily so the administrator can complete the review or you can submit a replacement. Access remains restricted to authorized administrators. Do not upload unrelated documents or images.

6. Acceptable use of the platform

You agree not to misuse the platform, attempt to access another person's account, bypass feature restrictions, upload malicious or unlawful content, interfere with platform operations, or copy, distribute or use learning materials in ways that violate applicable rights or university rules.

7. Account security and support

You are responsible for activity carried out through your account. If you suspect unauthorized access or encounter a problem, use the Contact Admin feature and provide enough information for the administrators to investigate. Password recovery instructions are sent to the email address associated with your account.

8. Platform availability and changes

Features and learning materials may change as UNI-VERSE is maintained and improved. Temporary interruptions may occur during maintenance or because of service-provider issues. These terms may be updated when platform processes change; material changes should be communicated through the platform.

9. Your acceptance

By selecting the acceptance checkbox on the registration page, you confirm that you have read and understood these Terms and Conditions, including the student ID verification process, the access restrictions for unverified accounts and the deletion of your uploaded ID image after approval.
